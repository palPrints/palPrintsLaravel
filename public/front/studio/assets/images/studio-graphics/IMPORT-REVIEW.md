### Provenance basis

Every published record preserves the exact supplied detail URL, actual SVG download URL, uploader name, explicit source-page CC0 license URL, review date/status, and relevant upstream evidence. The manifest is metadata only; the separate [machine-readable audit](import-review.json) covers all 60 supplied links and SHA-256 hashes of published assets.

The two historic-source entries (praying man, phoenix) document the uploader's specific named 1896/1910 work and CC0 declaration; these are recorded source statements, not independent archival/legal certifications.

Verified upstream releases include [GameArt2D's Free Assets License](https://www.gameart2d.com/license.html), [animal silhouettes](https://www.publicdomainpictures.net/en/view-image.php?image=455282&picture=animals-wildlife-zoo-wild-animal), [deer and girl](https://www.publicdomainpictures.net/en/view-image.php?image=387096&picture=deer-and-girl), and the dove's documented Openclipart parents. Approval denotes the source/asset editorial review; it is not a guarantee against unknown third-party claims.

## SVG safety and normalization

The reusable scripts/sanitize-studio-svg.ps1 parses XML without resolving external resources; rejects entities/internal DTDs, unsupported visual markup, remote/embedded resources, raster images, and missing local references; removes scripts, event attributes, metadata, editor namespaces, comments, and empty text. Simple class-only exported CSS is safely inlined; complex stylesheets are rejected rather than silently dropping intended paints. Allowed shapes, fills, strokes, gradients, local uses, clips, masks, and filters are preserved.

Validated finite, positive viewBoxes are retained/normalized. Canonical dimensions follow the reviewed viewBox, not editor width/height. Rendered alpha bounds were measured at a 2048-pixel maximum dimension, with three-pixel safety padding. Transparent whitespace was trimmed without rewriting artwork paths; aspect ratio is preserved.

Only these three individually reviewed background layers were removed:

- Orange classic car (342710): separate full-canvas gray first path.
- Vintage car (342707): separate full-canvas orange path866.
- Dolphin (176706): full-canvas off-white path3269.

The colored car arches, dolphin colors, flag composition, mosque sky, and rounded green icon remain intentional artwork. The poster background was ambiguous and was held rather than automatically removed.

No aggressive vector-node simplification was attempted: metadata/style cleanup and minification are lossless, and unnecessary geometric rewrites could change the artwork. No embedded raster content is treated as fully scalable. The 15.8 MB animal illustration is held for optimization rather than causing slow imports.

## Editor integration and performance

- Initial graphics context selects فلسطين. Only the active category's records are filtered/searched; no all-category startup thumbnail load.
- 24 thumbnails are rendered per batch, with lazy image loading and asynchronous decoding. Load-more appends the next batch without rebuilding existing cards.
- Assets are not loaded into Fabric until added (or restoring an already-persisted graphic). Only lightweight catalog metadata is read at startup.
- Published assets were SHA-256 checked; there are no duplicate SVG files.
- Clicking inserts centered at at most 30% print-zone width and 40% height, preserving aspect ratio. Very wide/tall graphics scale down automatically.
- Async insertion checks the area-switch token and measures the current print zone after loading, avoiding stale geometry.
- 16 reviewed monochrome graphics allow one-color recoloring using the existing Fabric tint filter; transparency is preserved. Multicolor graphics keep their colors.
- The existing normalized document persists graphicId, normalized position/dimensions, color, angle, and flips, not SVG source. Existing uploaded-asset storage remains unchanged.
- Existing movement snapping applies to image, text, and graphic objects. The closest print-zone edge/center within 6 screen pixels (6 divided by logical-to-screen scale) supplies a temporary guide and positional offset. No other objects are targets, no resizing/rotation snapping is added, and objects are never clamped to the zone.
